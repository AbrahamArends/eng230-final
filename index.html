ENG 230-800 Final Project

By Abe Arends

In this mini digital anthology, I aim to tie together a common theme between four texts written by women, three of which we read in class this semester.

I will explore how each of the below texts provide male characters that oppress and undermine their female protagonists, and how the women in each text find freedom or escape for themselves.

Trifles

Susanne Glaspell

The Glass Castle

Jeannette Walls

The Company of Wolves

Angela Carter

The Story of an Hour

Kate Chopin
